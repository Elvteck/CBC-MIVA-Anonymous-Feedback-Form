How this stays anonymous

  • Nothing asks who you are.There is no sign-in, name or email field, and the page keeps no draft on your device.
  • Only your words are sent.The email the club receives holds the message type, the area and your text.

One caution. In a small club, a very specific story or a familiar turn of phrase can give you away. Write about the issue, and keep personal details out.

About the club

Claude Builder Club Miva is a student community at Miva Open University for people who want to build with Claude. We meet to learn, try ideas, and turn them into working projects, whatever course you study.

The club belongs to its members. What you send through this page shapes our sessions, our projects and how we run things.
